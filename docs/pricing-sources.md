# Vehicle pricing sources

LuxDrive books test drives; it does not sell cars, so the **customer portal shows no prices** (no price on cards or vehicle pages, and no price filter or price sorting). The optional starting price is kept for staff only: it appears in the administration portal's vehicle list and vehicle form, where staff can change it or leave it empty.

The demo data stores a price only where a Pakistani price could be traced to a source; every other vehicle has no price (shown to staff as "Price on request"). Prices were researched in November 2026 and change often (the Finance Act 2026 changed import duties in July 2026), so treat them as a snapshot, not a quotation.
